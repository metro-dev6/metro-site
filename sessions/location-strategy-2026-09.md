**Geography:** Seven Oaks, Tevis Ranch, Southern Oaks, The Oaks, Haggin Oaks, and Terra Vista are in or immediately around zip 93311. Stockdale Estates is adjacent (93309/93311 border, ~5 miles southwest of downtown, near Stockdale Country Club and CSUB). Stone Meadows is a separate southwest pocket (zip 93313). All 8 are drivable in one tight loop — an operational plus, but it means no "different part of town" story exists between them. Each page's anchor has to come from character, not geography.

**Character anchors for page copy:**

- **Tevis Ranch / Southern Oaks** — newer construction (mostly 1970s-2000s), single-family homes, high owner-occupancy, top 15% family-friendly in California. 39% work executive/professional jobs. Anchor: busy family households, multiple vehicles, kids' sports gear and school runs.
- **Haggin Oaks / The Oaks** — median resident age 38, home prices $489K-$1.025M, "serene," established, picturesque, parks nearby (Haggin Oaks Park, University Park). Older and more settled than Tevis Ranch. Anchor: established homeowners who take pride in an older, nicer street.
- **Stockdale Estates** — adjacent to CSUB and Stockdale Country Club, upscale suburban, family- and professional-oriented. Anchor: proximity to the country club and university.
- **Stone Meadows** — largest population of the new additions (7,064), zip 93313, separate pocket from the 93311 cluster. Character/anchor detail not yet researched in depth — do this before writing the page.
- **Terra Vista** — zip 93311, same cluster as Tevis Ranch and Seven Oaks (shares Old River, Tevis Junior High schools). Existing page copy frames it as a "different part of town" from the other pages — that line is inaccurate and should be corrected, since it's part of the same cluster, not distant from it.

For scale: Rosedale's overall numbers already beat every southwest-8 neighborhood except Seven Oaks on income, and beat all 8 on home value. Shiloh Estates alone beats every neighborhood in either cluster on home value.

**Geography:** Shiloh Estates sits in zip 93314. Brimhall Estates, Nottingham Estates, and Riverlakes sit together in zip 93312. Northwest of the southwest cluster — a second drivable loop, separate trip from the southwest 8.

**Character anchors for page copy:**

- **Shiloh Estates** — biggest homes, most vehicles per household (34% own 4+), top-tier family-friendliness. Anchor: the multi-vehicle household that needs more than a driveway wipe-down.
- **Brimhall Estates** — custom-built, three-car garages, pools, big lots, built 1992-2001. Anchor: a serious personal investment in the home and cars to match — pool decks, garage-kept vehicles.
- **Nottingham Estates** — newest housing stock of the group (92.7% post-2000), explicitly family-rated. Anchor: young families in newer construction, similar pitch to Tevis Ranch/Southern Oaks on the southwest side but from a different, non-overlapping neighborhood.
- **Riverlakes (Riverlakes Ranch)** — lakefront, HOA-maintained landscaping, walking paths, Norris School District. Anchor: the lake and the HOA-kept curb appeal — a car that doesn't match the street sticks out here.
- **Rosedale (catch-all)** — needs a full rewrite from its current ~80-90 word placeholder. Should read as a general northwest Bakersfield overview (growing area, newer construction generally) and link to Riverlakes / Shiloh Estates / Brimhall Estates / Nottingham Estates for anyone whose specific neighborhood is named.

**Dropped from consideration:**
- **Rosedale West** — a NeighborhoodScout census-tract boundary, not a name people search or realtors use. No page.
- **Rosedale Ranch** — a proposed 1,650-acre industrial/logistics development, not a residential neighborhood. Unrelated, discard.
- **Masterpiece Estates** — real income number, but 23.6% childhood poverty rate in the same tract signals real inequality within it. Skipped.
- **Emerald Estates** — real income number, but NeighborhoodScout tags it "urban" with 32.4% in manufacturing/laborer occupations and 11.8% childhood poverty — weaker match to the ICP than the other four. Skipped.
- **Redwood Estates** — moderate income (61st percentile), thin anchor ("newer homes, longer commute"). Bench option if a 5th NW page gets added later, not part of the initial build.
