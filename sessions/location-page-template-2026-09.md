# Location Page Content Template — 2026-09

Two templates: named neighborhood pages and catch-all pages. Applies to all 14 pages in `location-strategy-2026-09.md`.

## Named neighborhood pages (12 pages: Seven Oaks, Terra Vista, Stockdale Estates, Southern Oaks, Tevis Ranch, The Oaks, Stone Meadows, Haggin Oaks, Riverlakes, Shiloh Estates, Brimhall Estates, Nottingham Estates)

The core risk: 8 of these sit in one drivable southwest loop, 4 more in a northwest loop. The page has to survive someone swapping the neighborhood name and asking "would this still make sense." Every section below should fail that test if you swapped in a different neighborhood's name.

**Title tag / H1** — neighborhood name + "mobile detailing," e.g. "Mobile Detailing in Shiloh Estates | Metro Auto Detailing." Title tag under 60 characters.

**Opening (no H2, this is the intro under the H1)** — Skip the generic "we come to you" line. Open with the real anchor from the location-strategy doc: home type, typical vehicle count, what the neighborhood is actually like. This is the sentence that makes the page distinct from its neighbors.

**Character section (H2)** — Built from the anchor data: home age, typical vehicle mix, family type, schools if relevant, HOA character. This section does the heavy lifting against thin/duplicate content. Needs to read like it was written by someone who's driven those streets, not filled in from a template.

**Services section (H2)** — 2-3 packages/add-ons that fit the neighborhood's anchor, not the full menu copy-pasted. Family-heavy newer-build areas point toward Interior Detail, Pet Hair, Carpet/Seat Extraction. Pool-and-garage areas (Brimhall Estates) point toward Full Detail, Engine Bay, Leather Conditioning. Keep pricing visible.

**Service commitment section (H2)** — No completed job required to write this. Ground it in real specifics instead: drive distance/time from base, confirmation the full mobile setup (water, power) works there, a concrete local reference (cross street, park, HOA name). Once a real job happens in that neighborhood, swap this section for the real story. Doesn't block launch either way.

**Nearby areas section (H2)** — Links sideways to the other named pages in the same cluster, and up to that cluster's catch-all page. Fixes the internal-linking gap from the SEO audit and signals to Google these are sibling pages, not duplicates.

**Local FAQ (H2)** — 2-3 hyper-local questions, e.g. "Do you serve gated communities in Shiloh Estates?" Feeds the FAQPage schema already used sitewide.

**Estimate CTA** — same as rest of site.

**Word count target:** 400-600 words, weighted toward the character and services sections, not a hard floor — the bar is genuine distinctiveness, not a number.

**Schema:** LocalBusiness/Service schema `areaServed` set to the specific neighborhood name, not just "Bakersfield."

## Catch-all pages (2 pages: Southwest Bakersfield, Rosedale)

Different job: broad-search net, not a competitor to the named pages under it. Keep it short so it doesn't compete with its own children for the same rankings.

**Title tag / H1** — area name + "mobile detailing," e.g. "Mobile Detailing in Southwest Bakersfield | Metro Auto Detailing."

**Opening** — General area overview. Broader brush than the named pages: growing area, general character, why Metro covers it.

**Neighborhoods we serve grid (H2)** — The most important element on this page. Links out to every named page in that cluster (Southwest Bakersfield links to Seven Oaks, Terra Vista, Stockdale Estates, Southern Oaks, Tevis Ranch, The Oaks, Stone Meadows, Haggin Oaks; Rosedale links to Riverlakes, Shiloh Estates, Brimhall Estates, Nottingham Estates).

**Services + estimate CTA** — same pattern as named pages, shorter.

**Word count target:** 250-350 words. If it starts saying everything the named pages say, it's competing with them instead of feeding them traffic.

## Per-page anchor status (fill in as pages are drafted)

Track which pages are running on place-specific detail alone vs. an actual completed job, so future copy updates know where to swap in a real story once one exists. Not a blocker for launch on either template.
