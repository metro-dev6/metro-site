# Stone Meadows — Character Anchor (researched 2026-09-09)

Fills the gap flagged in `location-strategy-2026-09.md` ("Stone Meadows character/anchor detail — not yet researched").

**Housing stock:** 95.5% of homes built after 1999, median year built 2006 — the newest housing stock in the southwest cluster. 99.3% single-family detached. Mediterranean/Tuscan-influenced architecture in places: terracotta tile roofs, stucco exteriors, some traditional shingled roofs. Short, straight streets with numerous cul-de-sacs. Standard two-car garages. Average home size ~1,770 sq ft, typical range 1,400-2,400 sq ft, lot size ~6,969 sq ft.

**Vehicles:** 37.5% of households own four or more vehicles — more than 97.3% of neighborhoods nationally. Highest vehicle-ownership rate of any neighborhood in either cluster (beats Shiloh Estates' 34%).

**The anchor:** exceptionally high vehicle ownership on short cul-de-sac streets with standard two-car garages. That combination means overflow vehicles regularly sitting in driveways and on the street, not tucked away. Real, specific, and different from every other southwest page — none of the others pair high vehicle counts with garage-space limits like this.

**Schools:** zoned to Ridgeview High School, ranked Niche's #1 standout high school in the Bakersfield area. Also Granite Pointe Elementary (B) and Stonecreek Junior High (B-minus). Fits the ICP's "people who want it done right" profile.

**Family fit:** top 6.1% family-friendly neighborhoods in California. Owner-occupied, low crime, low vacancy (1.7%).

**Income/value (already in the main strategy doc, repeated for reference):** $95,285 median household income, $269,567-$546,520 median home value depending on source (HomeSnacks vs. NeighborhoodScout), population 7,064, zip 93313.

**Suggested page angle:** lead with the driveway-overflow tension, not the income number. A family with four-plus vehicles and a two-car garage has cars sitting outside more than almost anywhere else in Bakersfield — that's the specific, real reason mobile detailing fits Stone Meadows particularly well.
